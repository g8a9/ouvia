Overview

In this study, you will review how a speech translation system performed on some voice recordings. We would like to understand your perspective on the translation's usability through three questions (detailed below). You will be asked to respond to these questions twice. First, based solely on viewing the translation (even if you do not speak Portuguese). Then, after seeing some additional information, namely a quality score (on a 0-100 scale, where 100 means perfect translation) given by a Portuguese speaker, and validation results showing whether a Portuguese receiver correctly understood the key information from your message, based on the translation they received.

Instructions

For each task assigned, listen carefully to the recording and review the translation provided by the AI system (for your convenience, we also provide the English transcript right below the audio player). Then, please answer the following three questions with your agreement on a 5-point scale (1 = Strongly Disagree, 5 = Strongly Agree).

  • Satisfaction: "I am satisfied with the quality of the AI translation."
  • Trust: "I trust the AI translation to convey my message."
  • Reliance: "I would use this AI translation in a real-world situation."
