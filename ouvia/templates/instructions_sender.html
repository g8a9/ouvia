Task Overview

In the first phase, your task is to record the provided English text in your own voice. These recordings will be used as the source input for a speech translation system. It is crucial that the recordings are clear and natural.

Environment and Equipment

  • Location: Please ensure an environment with minimal background noise.
  • Microphone: Use a consistent, good-quality microphone for all recordings. If using a computer's internal microphone, ensure you speak clearly towards it.
  • Device: Use the same recording device and software for all sessions to maintain consistency.

Recording Procedure

  • Familiarize Yourself with the Text: Read through the provided text to understand its content and flow before you start recording.
  • Speak Naturally: Read the text at a natural pace, as if you were speaking to another person. Avoid overly formal or robotic speech.
  • Clarity is Key: Enunciate your words clearly, but without exaggerating.
  • Read Without Changes: Read the text exactly as it is provided, without adding, omitting, or altering any words.
  • Review Your Recording: Listen to your recording to check for any audio issues. If you are not satisfied with the quality, please re-record.

Please note that once you hit “SAVE Recording”, you will not be able to re-record and can only proceed to the next conversation. Make sure to save only after reviewing the recording.
