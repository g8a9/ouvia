Upload Tips

  • conv_id groups rows into a single conversation. Every question sharing the same conv_id is attached to that conversation.
  • qid should be unique inside each conversation. Duplicate combinations of qid and question are skipped automatically.
  • unique_id must be unique across the entire platform—duplicates inside a CSV are skipped.
  • conv_entry_id values come from the conversation entry export and connect receiver/validator uploads to the correct sender conversation.
  • Roles must be one of sender, receiver, validator, or admin. Admin accounts still require a password set by the study team.
