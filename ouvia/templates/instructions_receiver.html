Task Overview

You will read short texts in Portuguese that have been translated from English by an AI system. After each audio clip, you will be asked a few multiple-choice questions in Portuguese about the content of the audio. Your task is to answer these questions based on your understanding of the translated audio.

Instructions

  • Read Carefully: Read the provided text carefully
  • Answer Questions Based on the Text: Answer the questions based only on the information you read in the Portuguese text. Do not use any prior knowledge you may have on the topic or infer information not available in the text provided.
  • Please note that the translation may not be perfect, as it is generated by an AI system. Whenever it is not possible to answer the questions, please indicate this with 'NA' in your responses.
