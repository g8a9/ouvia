Task Overview

You will be given an English audio clip and its transcript, a Portuguese translation of that audio, and answers written in Portuguese to comprehension questions about the audio.

  • 1) Audio / Transcript check: Perform a quick verification that the English transcript matches the audio. If you find major discrepancies (different facts, times, places, or missing key content), flag the mismatch but continue with the comprehension and translation assessments for that item.
  • 2) Comprehension check: Verify whether each Portuguese answer correctly and completely answers the question based on the English audio and transcript.
  • 3) Translation quality: Assess the overall quality of the Portuguese translation (scale described below).

Both the translation and the answers were produced by other participants.

Audio / Transcript Check

This is a brief verification step. Listen to the audio and skim the English transcript to confirm they convey the same information. If the audio omits or changes major facts (for example, different times, places, people, or outcomes) or otherwise does not match the text content, mark it as mismatched by ticking the checkbox.

Small differences that do not affect the meaning (e.g., filler words, brief disfluencies, or minor rephrasing) can be ignored.

Comprehension Questions

You will be shown comprehension questions in English with answers in Portuguese. For each item, mark whether the Portuguese answer is Correct (Yes) or Incorrect (No). When deciding, the following:

  • Audio relevance: Is the Portuguese answer factually correct with respect to the English audio clip? In other words, express your judgment based on the audio content, not the transcript.
  • Completeness: Does the answer fully address all parts of the question? Mark "Yes" only if it does.
  • Clarity and language: Is the answer clearly expressed in Portuguese (understandable, no major typos or grammar errors)? Mark "Yes" only if it is clear and readable.

If an answer is only partly correct or misses important information, select No.

Overall Translation Quality

How accurately does the Portuguese translation convey the meaning of the English audio?

Score the translation on a scale from 0 to 6. You can choose any score, including intermediate values. Here is an interpretation of the scale:

  • 6: Perfect Meaning and Grammar: The meaning of the translation is completely consistent with the English source. The grammar is also correct.
  • 4: Most Meaning Preserved and Few Grammar Mistakes: The translation retains most of the meaning of the source. It may have some grammar mistakes or minor contextual inconsistencies.
  • 2: Some Meaning Preserved: The translation preserves some of the meaning of the source but misses significant parts. The narrative is hard to follow due to fundamental errors. Grammar may be poor.
  • 0: Nonsense/No meaning preserved: Nearly all information is lost between the translation and source. Grammar is irrelevant.
Important: Once you submit your validation, it cannot be changed. Please make sure you have reviewed all materials carefully before clicking "Save validation".
